Brand Strategy

Phan tich doi thu, thi truong va tim ra noi dau tham kin cua khach hang (Customer Insight).

  • Dinh vi thuong hieu
  • Xay dung nen tang cot loi
  • Chuyen hoa chien luoc
  • Quan tri dai han

Thiet ke he thong nhan dien thuong hieu toan dien, nhat quan tren moi diem cham.

  • Logo & visual system
  • Color palette & typography
  • Brand guidelines
  • Ung dung thuc te (social, print, packaging)

Xay dung chien luoc content nhat quan, tang tuong tac va phat trien cong dong.

  • Lua chon nen tang phu hop
  • Content pillar & editorial calendar
  • Tone of voice tren social
  • KPI & do luong hieu qua

Dinh huong noi dung va hinh anh dong bo, tao an tuong nhat quan trong mat nguoi dung.

  • Visual concept & moodboard
  • Content format phu hop tung nen tang
  • Visual hierarchy cho feed/profile
  • Style guide cho team noi bo

Xac dinh ro rang khach hang muc tieu — de noi dung cham dung nguoi, dung luc.

  • Phan tich hanh vi & tam ly khach hang
  • Customer persona chi tiet
  • Phan khuc thi truong
  • Insight & pain point

Xay dung giong noi va tam ly marketing giup thuong hieu giao tiep hieu qua hon.

  • Tone of voice & brand personality
  • Mood & emotion trong tung noi dung
  • Marketing mindset thuc chien
  • Storytelling thuong hieu
AI in Branding

Su dung AI de len y tuong thuong hieu nhanh hon, da dang hon va co chieu sau hon.

  • AI brainstorm ten thuong hieu & concept
  • AI tao moodboard tu mo ta
  • AI phan tich doi thu tu dong
  • Prompt engineering cho branding

Viet caption, content social media va copy marketing nhanh hon voi AI.

  • Prompt viet caption theo tone thuong hieu
  • AI viet content plan hang thang
  • AI toi uu hook & CTA
  • Cach edit AI output cho tu nhien

Tao moodboard va visual concept chuyen nghiep bang AI trong vai phut.

  • Midjourney / ChatGPT cho visual
  • AI reference cho designer
  • Xay dung concept deck bang AI
  • Tich hop AI vao quy trinh thiet ke

Nghien cuu insight khach hang sau hon va nhanh hon nho AI.

  • AI phan tich comment & feedback
  • AI tom tat trend nganh hang
  • AI tao survey & phan tich ket qua
  • AI du bao hanh vi khach hang

Lap ke hoach content theo thang va quy bang AI — tiet kiem thoi gian, tang chat luong.

  • AI tao editorial calendar 30 ngay
  • AI phan loai content pillar
  • AI suggest hashtag & keyword
  • Workflow tu dong hoa lap ke hoach

Xay dung quy trinh sang tao tich hop AI — lam viec thong minh hon, nhanh hon.

  • Thiet lap AI workflow ca nhan
  • Ket noi cac tool AI voi nhau
  • Tu dong hoa task lap di lap lai
  • Toi uu output cho tung nen tang
